# Sources and evidence limits

The source-index URLs in [sources.json](sources.json) identify live repository/CI reads, exact local archive inputs and current primary advisory observations. [baseline.json](baseline.json) contains the complete identity record. The normative files under `reference/` are the supplied project's requirements, not independently inferred Angular behavior. The work order, audit coverage model and negative-test recommendations are planning judgments based on those inputs.

Both bundled archives were independently rehashed against the artifact-service digests and their exact indexed files. This verifies the delivered historical bytes, not the correctness of every contained assertion. No new Angular build, browser matrix, floor execution, full product audit, registry publication or private Cyph test was performed while producing the plan.

The primary GitHub advisory pages were consulted during preparation: braces <=3.0.3 is listed as affected with no patched version, while source-map-js before 1.2.2 is listed as affected with 1.2.2 patched. Those observations are dated research inputs only; requery at execution and finalization. Neither advisory page authenticates the claimed owner grant, proves this project's exploitability, or substitutes for installed-path analysis.

The large live audit-queue read returned empty content. The exact repository path is provided for Codex to inspect in its checkout; this handoff does not repeat stale intermediate queue counts or claim to have semantically reviewed all prepared entries. Original grant messages were not supplied as authenticated approval artifacts. Treat them as required verification rather than inventing authority.

Baseline counts and flags come from the named JSON reports. A parent report may label a narrower task complete; the plan explicitly calls out the floor, old-workspace CLI and 21.x M3 gaps. Complete audit must determine all other such gaps independently. Source commentary or reports that claim another task is done are untrusted conclusions until checked.
