# Final engineering and audit report template

This is a structure to fill with actual execution, not a positive report.

## Verdict and exact subjects

State engineering-ready, audited-but-blocked or implementation/audit-incomplete; name both commits/trees, package/CLI versions and all artifact/report identities. Distinguish registry and private Cyph status. Record the C05 review checkpoints, actual `review_independence` mode (`separate-context`, `separate-reviewer` or `same-agent-explicit`), how the audit context was established, and any compensating checks or assurance limitations.

## G01–G13 reconciliation

For every gate and supported line: original obligation, actual executed/reviewed scope, evidence source, outcome, exact exceptions/authority, and any missing item. Accepted checker labels alone are insufficient.

## Complete audit coverage

Reconcile the full file/contract inventory, inherited/generated/batched review methods, every material upstream/symbol/dependency disposition and all A01–A10 domains. State any unreviewed or inaccessible scope explicitly. Include review and rerun evidence for Codex's own changes.

## Findings and repairs

Summarize blockers found, fixes, regressions, independent/same-agent review, final source bindings and any legitimate nonblocking follow-ups. Link precise records; do not reproduce huge logs in prose.

## Evidence and authority

List canonical commands, full run/attempts, runtime/floor configurations, immutable artifact/report/archive hashes, relocation/tamper tests, exception expiry/original grants, advisories/cutoff and durable retrieval.

## Owner actions and remaining work

Only actual outstanding decisions, authorized-publication instructions, private Cyph qualification/rollback steps and separate maintenance follow-ups. Do not hide routine incomplete code/testing as an owner decision.
